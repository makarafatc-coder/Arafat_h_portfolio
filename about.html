About Me

A student, a designer, a builder of digital ideas.

Based in Feni, Bangladesh — working across design, digital projects and technical setups.

FeniBangladesh

Hi, I’m Arafat Hossain — a student and creative individual from Feni, Bangladesh. I spend most of my time designing visuals, exploring web and app ideas, and working with CCTV and technical setups.

I like work that is simple, clean, and useful. Whether it’s a logo that finally feels right, or a camera system that just works when you need it — that’s the kind of thing I enjoy building.

Right now I’m focused on growing as a designer, learning more about digital product development, and taking on freelance work that helps people and small businesses look and work better.
