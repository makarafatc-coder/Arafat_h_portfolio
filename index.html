About Me

A student, a designer, a builder of digital ideas.

I’m Arafat Hossain, a student and creative individual from Feni, Bangladesh. I spend most of my time designing visuals in Photoshop, exploring web and app ideas, and working with CCTV and technical setups.

I like work that is simple, clean and useful — whether it’s a logo that finally feels right, or a camera system that just works when you need it.
